How to read these numbers

Ratings. Offensive rating is points scored per 100 offensive possessions. Defensive rating is points allowed per 100 defensive possessions. Net rating is the difference. Higher net rating is better.

Shooting & possession. Effective FG% gives extra weight to made threes: (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA. Turnover rate is turnovers / offensive possessions. Assists per turnover divides offensive assists by turnovers.

Rebounding. Offensive and defensive rebound percentages use rebounds secured in that role divided by the corresponding rebound opportunities.

Context. A dash means the denominator is zero or a metric is unavailable. The API sorts its numeric values before the UI marks these as unavailable. Lineups smaller than five share possessions with other combinations; their counts must not be added together as a league total. These are descriptive on-court results, without opponent adjustment.
